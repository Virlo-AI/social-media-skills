# Sound scout: consumer brand on TikTok · week of Nov 23 2026

**The short version:** two of the fastest-growing cleared sounds this week are the same meme, "in this town everybody goes out partying except me… HUSS".
- **Use the remix** (@djfunkyehh, 20 s): 422 videos this week, 80.6× its normal weekly rate, and a 19M-follower K-pop group posted it yesterday.
- **The original story audio** (@larpniverse, 75 s) is bigger but may be past its fastest day: TikTok's daily count of new uses peaked on Nov 21 and has slowed for two days.

- **Your version:** the team dancing the remix in the warehouse or studio while everyone else has left: "everybody goes out partying except us" over the busiest week of the year.
