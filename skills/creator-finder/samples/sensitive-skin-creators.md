### @ohwowitsjenny: maybe (closest to yes)
- **Consistent:** her 15 latest posts (Oct 18 – Nov 23) landed between 63,000 and 1.0M views. Typical views of 357,800 is 9.6× her followers, and the library median (383,876) agrees.
- **Sponsored, with likes intact:** all 15 posts carry AD, a partner tag or an ambassador tag, and their like rates run 2.03–4.98%. Example: [1.0M views and 30,000 likes](https://www.tiktok.com/@ohwowitsjenny/video/7703519489962347808).
- **On brief:** her [barrier-care post](https://www.tiktok.com/@ohwowitsjenny/video/7708714433610009888) ("barrier care doesn't end at our face") got 276,200 views (0.76× outlier) and 4,009 saves, a 1.45% save rate, the best on any page read. An [Ectoin and panthenol barrier cream post](https://www.tiktok.com/@ohwowitsjenny/video/7701665064381582624) ran at 1.04×.
- **Claims:** 4 of 15 captions state a skin outcome, for example "works EVERYTIME 🙌🏾".
- **Check:** she tags three posts as an Anua ambassador, and a caption speaks to "my UK girlies", which suits a US/UK brand. Ask about exclusivity before briefing a competing barrier product.
- **Brief them on:** a barrier-care routine that goes past the jaw (neck, hands), in her own words, with approved wording agreed before filming.

**Method:**
- **One creator search** for "sensitive skin | skin barrier | skincare": individuals on TikTok with 10K–500K followers, sorted by top performing (`.cache/20261123-124807-v1-search-creators.json`). It returned 25 of 1,651; every filter was applied.
- **Shortlist:** 5 creators, by library median against followers, on-topic videos and breakout count, after dropping anyone whose last post was before Oct 12.
- **Vetting:** each on their live page of 15 latest posts (`.cache/20261123-1248*-v1-raw-tiktok-creator-*-videos.json`). Live typical views = what the page's posts typically get. "Sponsored" counts captions with AD, #ad, Anzeige, a discount code, a commission line, or a partner or ambassador tag; "tag a brand" counts captions that @-mention one. A skin outcome is a caption stating what a product did to skin.
- **Verdicts follow the skill's bands:** a yes needs no captions stating results, so creators whose only doubt is claims are a maybe.

**Spend:** $0.10 (search $0.05, 5 live pages at $0.01), from `virlo.sh spend`.
