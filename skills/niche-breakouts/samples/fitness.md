## What to do with this
1. **Film the class you teach, handheld.** Film it: phone in hand inside a real session, you leading with a mic or out loud, about 30 s, with the one fact that makes this session different on screen. Open with: "[Fact about today], and I'm still leading the 6am class." Built on: handheld vlogs, climbing from 11.7% to 25.6% of posts at 0.92× (not under); @abbey_weatherby at 209× outlier, with 7,926 comments.
2. **Show the months, in outfits.** Film it: one clip per month of the same lift or the same mirror, in that month's gym outfit, about 17 s, with the month count on screen. Open with: "[N] months ago I couldn't do one of these." Built on: outfit showcase, climbing from 2.7% to 3.8% at 0.89× (not under); @daniiilifts at 95× outlier.
3. **Tease the story in one line of text.** Film it: two short clips, before and now, with one line on screen that hints at who stood by you, about 8 s. Open with: "The person who trained beside me at the start is still here." Built on: story-tease hooks, climbing from 7.2% to 10.1% at 0.93× (not under); @gisss.ellle at 104× outlier.

**Stop:** talking-head videos (0.77×) and "here's my routine" tutorial promises (0.77×, the most common hook) are under. Nothing is crowded.
