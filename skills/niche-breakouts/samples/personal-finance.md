## What to do with this
1. **Open with a shock statement about a money feature you found late.** Film it: 20–25 s, camera in selfie view in a shop or at home, the line as big on-screen text over your face, then cut to a screen recording of the feature. Open with: "Eight years of online banking and nobody mentioned this?!" Built on: shock statement, index 1.42 and climbing; @tapsheet.marg's 41× Reel (see the like-rate caveat in row 8).
2. **Screen-record a budget dare.** Film it: about 1 minute. Set up the dare to camera in 5 seconds, then screen-record the shopping list or cart being built until it lands under the number. Open with: "Can forty dollars cover a week of lunches for two of us? Watching the total live." Built on: screen recording, climbing from under 3.6% to 5.7% of posts (index 0.92); @miranda.cooks5's 2,275×. It's a one-off: her 15 repeats got 6 to 692 views, so borrow the dare, not her playbook.
3. **One savings rule, 11 seconds, on screen.** Film it: 11 s, you to camera saying nothing much while the rule sits as on-screen text, one rule per video, posted often. Open with: "Payday rule: the bills get covered, then the rest disappears into savings." Built on: @brennan_valeski's repeatable formula, 513× here with 2 other 11-second rule posts at 12× and 1,705× (tutorial promise, index 0.98, is neutral on its own: the formula is his, not the hook type's).

**Stop:** no format or hook type is under 0.8 this month, so there's nothing to drop outright. The weakest are talking head (0.86, its share down from 53.4% to 48.4%) and question hooks (0.87): don't count on a plain talking head or an open question alone. @ricky.cpa's 817× question worked because it asked for comments, and got 1,812.

## Caveats

- **Platform mix:** 476 of 537 breakouts are on TikTok, 39 on Shorts and 22 on Reels.
- **Low like rate in typical views:** @clearthinkingclub and @tapsheet.marg have 3 or more posts on their page with likes under 0.5% of views, so their typical views aren't all organic reach.
- **Pinned posts:** @tapsheet.marg's page starts with 3 pinned Reels. They're out of its typical views and counted in its repeat check.
- **Stored vs live views:** 5 of the 8 breakouts weren't on the creator's latest page, so their views are Virlo's stored count.
- **Sample size:** the measured table is 8 creators, one page each (7 to 15 posts). The niche-wide claims come from the overviews.
