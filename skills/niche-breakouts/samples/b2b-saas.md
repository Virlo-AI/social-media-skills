## What to do with this
1. **"N ways your [product category] gets you sued, hacked or billed", as a list to camera.** Film it: 25–40 s, you to camera at home or at a desk, each item as a short on-screen line with its consequence, playing the attacker or the lawyer. Open with: "Shipped your first AI feature? Here's what the first angry customer will try." Built on: talking head, index 1.33 and steady (under-supplied); @ryannaghib's 55× and @murphmaxxing's 23×, two creators with repeatable versions of the same list.
2. **A sales or GTM leader in one sharp clip.** Film it: 30–70 s, two chairs or a recorded video meeting, cut to the one opinion that contradicts how SaaS teams sell today, with captions. Open with: "Our old sales team had four roles per deal. We need one now. Here's why." Built on: @_crewgtm's repeatable formula, 13× here with 2 other clips at 11× and 13× (the interview format itself has too few breakouts to index).
3. **Company stories, told as a titled series.** Film it: 70–80 s, one company's rise or fall per video, archive clips or screenshots with on-screen text, the same title pattern every time, ideally a SaaS company's. Open with: "SaaS companies that lost their best customers to a free plan." Built on: story tease, index 3.29 and climbing; @business.explained1's repeatable "Companies that…" series, 8.7× here with 2 others at 11× and 12×.

**Stop:** animation and motion-graphics explainers. Their share of B2B SaaS posts went from 3.2% to 12.6%, and none of the 10 breakouts uses one: crowded. Many of them are agencies selling product videos.
