# What's breaking out in skincare
Oct 24 – Nov 23 2026 · TikTok, Reels, Shorts · any region

**The answer:** Skincare's biggest breakouts this month came from tiny accounts posting honest verdicts, hands-on cheat sheets, before/afters and questions to the comments, not sale picks.
- **7 breakouts measured** ran at 190× to 4,207× outlier; an eighth is unmeasured because the video is gone from the account. All 8 creators have under 13K followers.
- **Paid posts:** 8 of the top 25 recent posts are labelled paid, 4 of them Sephora Savings Event picks. They earned 0.14% likes per view; the 17 organic ones earned 6.10%.
- **The clearest opening is the hands-on demo.** It's 11.0% of breakouts but only 5.7% of posts, and its share of posts has nearly halved since Jul–Oct.

## What to do with this
1. **Make the hands-on cheat sheet.** Film it: hands only over a printed chart on a table, one concern per row with the ingredient for it, about 25 s, no face needed. Open with: "Your blackheads and your red bumps want different things." Built on: activity demonstration, index 1.93× and fading in supply (under-supplied); @dauixgpzgzu's cheat sheet at 1,897× outlier, with 2 more posts at 10×+.
2. **Ask the comments one question.** Film it: a selfie in your bedroom or bathroom, the question as big stacked text, no talking, about 12 s. Open with: "Okay skincare people: what's the weirdest thing that ever calmed your breakouts?" Built on: question hooks, climbing from 7.2% to 9.0% of posts at 1.17×; @nikkidoesskincare's question at 606× outlier, with 3,016 comments.
3. **Post the day-count diary.** Film it: your routine in the bathroom, talked through as an update on day [N] of the same products; the source runs 10 minutes. Open with: "Day [N] of the same four products, so here's the honest update." Built on: story-tease hooks, climbing from 8.8% to 12.5% of posts at 0.90× (not under); @annaflynt7's day-34 skin-flooding update at 272× outlier, a one-off on her page.

**Stop:** product close-ups as the main shot (0.70×, climbing from 7.7% to 13.3%) and relatable-scenario hooks (0.68×, climbing from 4.7% to 7.9%) are crowded; "10-step routine" tutorial promises are the most common hook at 0.72×.

**For a brand account:** @grahamsnatural's founder story broke out once (1,716×), but the 5 posts since got 213 to 1,223 views. Treat a story breakout as reach to follow up with more of the story, not as proof the product posts will carry.

## Caveats
- **Older than the page:** 5 of the 8 breakouts (#1, #2, #5, #7 and the unmeasured #8) predate the creator's live page, so their outlier compares stored views with typical views of later posts (dates in the table).
- **The outlier table is a sample:** 8 creators, one live page each (14–15 recent posts). The niche-wide claims come from the overviews.
- **Pinned posts:** no page had a post at the top that was far older than the rest, so none was left out as probably pinned.
- **Platform mix:** 88% of matches are TikTok (5,789). Shorts (502) and Reels (305) are thin in this search, and all 8 measured breakouts are on TikTok.
- **Labels:** formats and hook types are Virlo's AI labels, and many videos have no hook type.
- **Filters:** every filter asked for was applied (`not_applied` was empty on all three searches).
