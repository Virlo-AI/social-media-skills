## 1. What does your face never fight? · 12 s · selfie, text on screen
**Built on:** [@nikkidoesskincare's question to "acne girls"](https://www.tiktok.com/@nikkidoesskincare/video/7702098491606043935), 686K views, 606× outlier, 3,016 comments, 12 s. Selfie in her bedroom, the question as stacked on-screen text, no speech.
**What we borrow:** one question in big stacked text, the creator's face holding still under it, and the caption asking for help.

| Time | On screen | Voice / action | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | sensitive skin people… | Founder looks into the lens, no talking | Selfie, eye level, bathroom |
| 3–7 s | what's the one product / your face never fights? | A small shrug; she lifts an empty hand, palm up | Selfie |
| 7–10 s | (mine's [product type]) | She holds up one bottle, label to camera | Selfie, bottle near the lens |
| 10–12 s | tell me below 👇 | She points down at the comments | Selfie |

- **Shot list:**
  1. Phone at eye level, window light, the founder's own bathroom, no ring light.
  2. One bottle within reach, any brand, the label readable.
  3. Text in three stacked lines, large, centred in the top half so the face stays clear.
- **Caption:** My skin hates almost everything. What's the one thing yours is fine with?
- **Hashtags:** #sensitiveskin #skintok #skincarehacks #skincareroutine
- **Comment loop:** the answers are the content. Reply-video the most-liked answer: "[Commenter's product type] came up 40 times, so let's look at why it's gentle."
- **Claims check:** "never fights" is the viewer's experience, not a claim about a product. In the reply video, say what the ingredient is for; never "safe for all sensitive skin". The count in the reply ("40 times") must be a real count.
- **Don't copy:** "acne girls", her headband, her bedroom, her mention of a label-scanning app, and her caption.

## 2. Estheticians, settle this · 13 s · selfie, text on screen
**Built on:** [@alaynachristineee's call to estheticians](https://www.tiktok.com/@alaynachristineee/video/7705998022311693581), 2.6M live views, 46× outlier, 3,000 comments, 13 s. Selfie, the call-out as one long line of on-screen text, no speech. Her caption promises to follow the advice.
**What we borrow:** a call-out to one profession in the first line, a real reason to ask, and a promise in the caption to do what they say.

| Time | On screen | Voice / action | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | estheticians, I need you 🙋‍♀️ | Founder raises a hand like calling a waiter | Selfie, eye level |
| 3–8 s | is [ingredient] ever okay on reactive skin? | She holds two bottles, one in each hand, and tilts her head | Selfie, both bottles in frame |
| 8–11 s | we've gone back and forth for [time] | She puts one bottle down, then picks it back up | Selfie |
| 11–13 s | top comment decides | She points at the camera | Selfie |

- **Shot list:**
  1. Same setup as script 1: eye level, window light, no filter.
  2. Two real bottles, one with the ingredient and one without, labels turned out.
  3. One line of text at a time; nothing over the face.
- **Caption:** Genuinely asking. We'll post what you tell us, even if it's not what we want to hear.
- **Hashtags:** #sensitiveskin #esthetician #skincare #skinbarrier
- **Comment loop:** experts answer, everyone else argues. The follow-up is the founder reading the top three expert answers and saying what the brand will do with them.
- **Claims check:** the question must be one the brand really hasn't settled, and "[time]" a real length of time. The follow-up quotes commenters only as written and makes no claim on their behalf.
- **Don't copy:** her age, the "I look 55" story, her "humbled" framing and her hashtags.

## 3. Four kinds of sensitive · 25 s · hands only, overhead
**Built on:** [@dauixgpzgzu's acne cheat sheet](https://www.tiktok.com/@dauixgpzgzu/video/7705843182390480141), 2.6M views, 1,902× outlier, 26 s. Hands-only tabletop demo of a printed "Acne Cheat Sheet" chart with product images, tripod, natural light. Its caption asks viewers to save it and say which type they have.
**What we borrow:** a printed chart, one row per type, hands working down it row by row, then save and comment asks at the end.

| Time | On screen | Voice / action | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | 4 kinds of sensitive | The blank chart is laid on the table | Overhead, tripod, daylight |
| 2–6 s | RED → look for [A] · skip [B] | Hand places the two ingredient cards in row 1 | Overhead |
| 6–10 s | TIGHT → [A] · skip [B] | Row 2 | Overhead |
| 10–14 s | STINGING → [A] · skip [B] | Row 3 | Overhead |
| 14–18 s | FLAKY → [A] · skip [B] | Row 4 | Overhead |
| 18–22 s | [product] goes here only | One product slides into the single row it's for | Overhead, slow push in |
| 22–25 s | which row are you? save it | Hand taps the chart, then lifts it toward the lens | Overhead to angled |

- **Shot list:**
  1. A4 chart printed in your own design on a matte table, overhead on a tripod, window light.
  2. Ingredient cards as plain text; no product images on them.
  3. Only products that belong in a row appear; rows with none of yours stay empty.
  4. The chart lifted toward the lens for the last beat.
- **Caption:** Red, tight, stinging or flaky: find your row before you buy anything.
- **Hashtags:** #sensitiveskin #skinbarrier #skincaretips #skincare
- **Comment loop:** "which row are you?" Reply-video the most common answer with a slower walk through that one row.
- **Claims check:** every [A] and [B] needs sign-off from your formulator or a dermatologist. "Look for" and "skip" are guidance; no "clears", "heals" or "fixes". Analysis flagged the source for medical claims, so keep yours to what ingredients are for.
- **Don't copy:** her "Acne Cheat Sheet" title, her layout, her product images and her ingredient picks.
