## Moves for you
1. **Hand the camera to a real customer.** Post it: a person with a reactive-skin condition walks through what they use and why, in their own words, tagged as a partner. Opening line: "I've had [their condition] for [years]; here's the only routine my skin doesn't fight." · Built on: CeraVe's creator-partner posts at 2.82× and 1.50× (2 of its 15 posts) and Rhode's person-led posts at 1.64× on 4; person-led beats product-led in 2 of the 2 accounts that post it more than once. · Instead of: a brand tie-in that runs on and on (CeraVe's Dune posts, a third of its page, at 0.31×).
2. **Post condition tips, not product pushes.** Post it: one concern per video (stinging after moisturiser, flaky patches), with the tip first and a product only if it belongs. Opening line: "If your cheeks sting after moisturiser, check this before you blame the cream." · Built on: Graham's Natural's condition tips at 1.43× on 6 posts, led by 5.94× and 3.13× · Instead of: product posts, 0.23× on 6 of its 15.
3. **Give the founder a regular slot.** Post it: the same weekday every week, the founder on camera with what she's testing on her own skin. Opening line: "[Weekday] with [founder's first name]: what's on my face this week, and what came off it." · Built on: Rhode's "THURSDAY WITH HB" at 2.58× and "FRIDAY WITH HB" at 2.00×, beating the launch drops posted the same days (0.88×). Lead on 1 account. · Instead of: back-to-back drop announcements.
