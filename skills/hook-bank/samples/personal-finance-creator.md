# Hook bank: budgeting creator · Nov 23 2026

**The finding:** On TikTok finance, the hooks that hit aren't the common ones.
- **POV setups hit strong 22.0% of the time** (1,116 videos), nearly twice the rate of bold claims.
- **Bold claims are the most used**, 28.7% of all finance hooks (27,380 videos), and hit strong only 11.7% of the time. Tutorial promises, the second most used (22.5%), hit 12.5%.
- Your six lean on POV setups, relatable scenarios and story teases, the three best types with real budgeting sources behind them.

## Avoid
- **Bold claims and tutorial promises as your default opener:** together they're 51.2% of finance hooks and hit strong 11.7% and 12.5%, against 22.0% for POV setups.
- **Sound templates:** "you look tired / i am okay / what are you even chasing" ([8.8M](https://www.tiktok.com/@realedgee/video/7701560907469573409)) opens 6 posts, and "I think I'm falling high, I'm in the eye of a hurricane" ([783K](https://www.tiktok.com/@isabella.invests/video/7706929195896655135)) opens 3. Both are lyrics that work with their sound, not lines to copy.
- **Supermarket "method" hooks:** the LIDL one (898K views, from a 1.7K-follower account) has likes on 0.25% of views, a low like rate, so it isn't counted as a hook that worked.
