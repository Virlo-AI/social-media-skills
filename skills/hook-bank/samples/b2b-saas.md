# Hook bank: B2B SaaS, founder-led · Nov 23 2026

**The finding:** In business and career content on TikTok, the hooks that hit are scenes from work, not advice about it.
- **Relatable scenarios, shock statements and POV setups** each hit strong about a quarter of the time (25.1%, 25.0%, 24.1%).
- **Tutorial promises and bold claims are nearly half of all business hooks** (23.7% and 23.3%) and hit 10.8% and 14.3%. The tutorial promise is the most used type and the one that hits least.
- Hooks that name SaaS are rare at scale: only 9 English business hooks with 100K+ views mention it, and the best of them is a POV ("POV: You're trying to cancel a SaaS subscription", VS 33.4).

## Avoid
- **Tutorial promises as the default opener:** the most used business hook (23.7%) and the one that hits least (10.8%), against 25.1% for relatable scenarios. Bold claims (23.3% of hooks) hit 14.3%.
- **Sound templates:** "No one talks about how hard it is to go from this..." opens [8 posts](https://www.tiktok.com/@daweiawa/video/7704647374676725025), and "Oh, you don't have to waste your night" opens 5. They work with their sound, not as lines.
- **SaaS-bashing lines without a turn:** the SaaS hooks that reach 100K+ mock vendors ("a little gaslighty") and cancel flows. Use them only when the video shows yours is the exception.
