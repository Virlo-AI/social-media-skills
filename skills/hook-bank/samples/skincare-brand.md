## Avoid
- **Tutorial promises as the opener:** the most used beauty hook (24.4% of all hooks) hits strong 21.2% of the time, against 29.1% for shock statements.
- **Template lines** like "my skin before / korean skincare:" (6 posts open with it) only work with their sound and trend; don't use them as writing.
- **"Guaranteed" and cure-style openers** such as "Glass skin Guranteed": they're result claims, which the brief rules out.

- **Dropped from the 50 read:**

  - off-topic for a skincare brand: hair, wigs, lashes, nails, waxing, makeup scars and advent calendars, unless the structure was the point;
