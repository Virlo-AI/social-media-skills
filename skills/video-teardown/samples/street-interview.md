## Why it worked
1. **The hook is the clip's most surprising line, pulled forward:** it's said at 0.1 s and again at 30.7 s, so the end loops into the start.
2. **The routine undercuts the face:** "It's nothing flashy." (9.0–11.6 s) and "soap and water" (20.4–22.1 s) from someone viewers guessed at 40.
3. **The payoff isn't a product** ("Happiness.", 23.5–25.5 s), so the video never feels like an ad; the products got about 100 comment likes against 34,072 for her looks.
4. **It's share-led:** 1.25% shared against 0.89% saved. People sent it to someone rather than keeping it for the routine.
5. **It's a format the account repeats:** 2 more interviews this month at 33× and 17× outlier.

## Your remix
This format needs two people and a place to meet them: a market stall or pop-up, with real customers who agree to be filmed. That's more than the brief's selfie and tabletop setup.

| Time | Beat | Line / on screen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Their surprise line, first | "[the most surprising true thing about them, in their words]" · on screen: the same line | Handheld, two-shot, outdoors |
| 3–6 s | The question | Founder, off camera: "What does your skin actually put up with?" | Handheld on the customer |
| 6–17 s | Their routine | [their two or three products, in their own words, and one plain remark about them] | Handheld, product shown only if they brought it |
| 17–21 s | What they gave up | Founder: "Anything you've stopped using?" → [their answer] | Handheld |
| 21–25 s | The non-product payoff | Founder: "And the real secret?" → [their answer, whatever it is] | Handheld, closer |
| 25–28 s | Who they are | Founder: "First name, and which city?" → [first name, city, age if they offer it] | Handheld |
| 28–34 s | The loop | Their surprise line again, cut to match the first frame | Same framing as 0–3 s |

**Openings to test:**
1. Question: "What's the one thing your skin has never once complained about?" (asked to camera, then cut to the answer)
2. Bold claim: "[Number] years of reactive skin, and her whole routine is [number] things." (on screen over the first frame)

**Don't copy:** the woman's name, age, relationship and career; her exact lines; the account's caption and "BeautyStreet" sound. Real customers only, never staged, with a signed release; don't put product claims in their mouths.
